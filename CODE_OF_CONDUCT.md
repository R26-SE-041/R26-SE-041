# Code of conduct

BioLearnX contributors should help maintain a respectful, welcoming environment
in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully; give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experiences, backgrounds and levels of technical knowledge.
- Protect other people's privacy and ask before sharing their personal information.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct inaccurate or harmful material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks and
publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated
disruption of project discussions is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact repository maintainers privately to report a concern. If no private
contact channel is listed, ask a maintainer for one without posting identifying
details or the incident publicly.

Maintainers review concerns in context and may ask for corrections, remove
inappropriate content or restrict participation. Treat reports and the identities
of people involved confidentially, sharing details only as necessary to resolve
the concern.
