# Manual visual audit of the fixed-seed smoke run

This audit checks only whether each image visibly matches the requested canonical view or section. It is not a full anatomical-accuracy review and was not blinded. The images are the ten PNGs in `images/` generated by protocol `paper-component-comparison-v1` with seed `260901`.

| Organ | Required view | Raw FLUX.1-dev | Our pipeline | Observation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heart | Anterior cutaway | Fail | Fail | Both images show an exterior heart surface rather than exposed chambers. |
| Brain | Midsagittal | Pass | Fail | Raw shows a midsagittal section with internal structures; pipeline shows an external lateral surface. |
| Kidneys | Paired coronal cutaway | Fail | Fail | Both images show paired external kidneys without an exposed coronal section. |
| Liver | Inferior visceral | Partial / uncertain | Fail | Raw exposes several visceral structures but is not an unambiguous inferior view; pipeline is dominated by an external surface and includes neighboring tissue. |
| Lungs | Airway cutaway | Pass | Pass | Both images visibly expose the branching airway tree, although this does not verify every required lobe or relation. |

## Evaluator validity finding

Qwen2.5-VL assigned every image a visual score of 9/10 and pedagogical score of 9/10. It also reported perfect or near-perfect structure recall and no hard failures, including for clear view mismatches above. Therefore the Qwen scores in `metrics-table.md` are model outputs, not validated anatomical ground truth.

The pipeline generation prompts recorded in `results.json` identify the canonical structured views correctly in `anatomy_spec.view`, but the compiled FLUX text reduces the requested view to the vague phrase `anatomical viewpoint or section: internal`. This explains why the pipeline can lose the required section even though its structured specification is correct. The relevant implementation path is `anatomy/loader.py::extract_requested_view` to `anatomy/prompt_builder.py::compile_anatomy_prompt`.

Do not use this smoke run to claim pipeline superiority. A paper-ready study needs a corrected canonical-view prompt, multiple fixed seeds, a held-out prompt set, and blinded independent expert scoring.
